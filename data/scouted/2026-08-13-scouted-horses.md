## Coverage Gaps / Data Reliability Notes

- **Irish and UK maiden/novice sires were mostly not obtainable.** irishracing.com, Racing Post, and Timeform did not surface sire/damsire in a form usable via WebFetch for the vast majority of Irish and UK maiden runners this cycle (Timeform returned a 403). The top sire/damsire criterion could only be systematically applied to **USA** maiden races, where Horse Racing Nation's entries pages include sire in-line — several qualifying USA horses were found this way (see "via criterion: top sire/damsire" rows above, including three horses — Flight Commander, Dark Force/Holy Seven/Bedeviled/Into Forever/Melody Man/Top Thriller/Free Verse/McCoy/Q B Rocket/Vukota — that qualify on pedigree alone with no followed trainer attached). One Irish exception: Oklahoma's sire (Wootton Bassett) came from a secondary news source, not the racecard itself, so it's flagged as moderate confidence rather than fully verified.
- **Dundalk (Sat 15) Race 5, a 29-runner 2yo maiden, was only partially re-verified this cycle.** Both irishracing.com and Racing Post returned "key contenders" style partial lists rather than full fields for this race. One Number, Oklahoma, Jesus Green, Wo Wo Never, and Aachen were independently confirmed present; Ile de France, Highest Order, True Sovereign and Speakers Corner are carried over from a prior (2026-08-11) check and flagged as lower confidence — recommend a direct re-check nearer the day.
- **Tramore (Sat 15) handicap races (4 through 7) only returned runner counts, not full names**, from the source used — so trainer-follow checks could not be completed for those four races. Races 1–3 were fully checked.
- **"Dropping in class off a Group run" (Top Tier) and USA "MSW-to-maiden-claimer" (Top Tier) criteria were not exhaustively checked** across every runner in every relevant race this cycle — would require individual past-performance lookups at a scale not feasible with available tools in the time available. - **USA Sunday 16 Aug entries (Saratoga, Del Mar) were not yet posted** as of this research — both tracks are confirmed racing that day (Saratoga's Grade 3 Mahony Stakes; Del Mar's Rancho Bernardo Stakes) but detailed entries with trainers/jockeys were unavailable, so Sunday US coverage beyond what's shown above (Baffert's Saturday-card runners, which land in the small hours of Sunday Irish time) is incomplete.
